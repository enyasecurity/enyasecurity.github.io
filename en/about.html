About Enya Security

Enya Security helps small and medium-sized businesses, professional practices and associations improve their cybersecurity through clear, educational and non-intrusive audits.

About the founder

My name is Marina Bruno. I hold a bachelor's degree in computer systems engineering and a master's in engineering management, and I founded Enya Security.

For several years I have worked with organisations on information system analysis, incident resolution, technical documentation and business process improvement. That experience shaped a rigorous, methodical approach focused on finding workable solutions.

Cybersecurity is where that work led me. I have built my skills through specialist training, hands-on labs and recognised certifications, including Ethical Hacking Essentials (EC-Council) and Pre-Security (TryHackMe), and I keep studying to stay current with how threats and good practice evolve.

I founded Enya Security with a simple goal: to make cybersecurity audits accessible to smaller organisations through an approach that is clear, understandable and non-intrusive.

Every audit is independent, confidential and pragmatic. The aim is not only to identify vulnerabilities but to explain what they mean for you and to propose recommendations that match your actual resources.
